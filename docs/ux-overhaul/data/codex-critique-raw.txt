1. **SEVERITY: HIGH — The proposal rewards compliance with bedtime, then calls it a ritual.** A streak makes a missed evening a failure; the seven-night surprise turns finishing books into a target. That creates pressure precisely when a parent may need to stop early. The rules also conflict: the vision promises a star every evening, A promises a gold star after seven consecutive evenings, and B promises an unspecified surprise after seven stars. **Evidence:** [VISION.md](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/VISION.md:16), [a-home.html](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/mockups/a-home.html:108), [b-home.html](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/mockups/b-home.html:72). **Fix:** Remove streaks, countdowns and locked surprises. If families want a memory, offer an optional, non-judgmental collection of reading moments with no penalty for gaps or stopping midway.

2. **SEVERITY: HIGH — “Child mode” is a label, not a safety design.** A press-and-hold exit can be learned or triggered accidentally; it cannot establish that an adult is present, and it must not be represented as security. The proposal does not define access to operating-system controls, external links, notifications, purchases, microphone or generated content when a child holds the phone alone. **Evidence:** [VISION.md](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/VISION.md:16), [VISION.md](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/VISION.md:71). **Fix:** Make child mode a deliberately restricted reading surface: no purchasing, sharing, open text, external links or microphone; use an adult gate for sensitive actions and state clearly that it is a deterrent, not device-level security. Require adult review of newly generated stories and images before child mode.

3. **SEVERITY: HIGH — The proposal has no response to an unsafe or upsetting story.** A personalised story can mishandle fear, violence, family loss, separation, body image or a child’s name and appearance. “Age 3–8” is too broad to define safe tone, and page-by-page revelation can expose unreviewed material. **Evidence:** The creation and immediate-reading flow in [VISION.md](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/VISION.md:63) specifies no review, report, edit or fallback state. **Fix:** Give parents age-appropriate defaults and optional sensitivities, screen text and images, allow a quick parent preview, and provide “skip this page,” edit, report and replace with a previously approved story. Test failure cases with parents and child-development expertise.

4. **SEVERITY: HIGH — The photo promise is materially misleading.** The mockup says the picture “stays only with you” and is deleted after drawing. The product’s own policy says the browser sends it to Sipurai’s server and a drawing provider, whose retention the product does not control. **Evidence:** [a-wizard.html](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/mockups/a-wizard.html:80), [privacyPolicyContent.js](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/src/data/privacyPolicyContent.js:263). **Fix:** Replace the claim with a short, verified explanation naming the categories of recipients, what Sipurai retains, what it cannot promise about providers, and how to delete the resulting character. Keep a photo-free path equally prominent. Verify the actual implementation before making any retention claim.

5. **SEVERITY: HIGH — “Private link for grandma” is undefined access control.** A link that opens only one book can still be forwarded, indexed, retained or opened by anyone who obtains it. It may expose a child’s name, appearance and family details. **Evidence:** [VISION.md](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/VISION.md:74). **Fix:** Make sharing a parent-only, explicit action with a clear audience preview, unguessable book-specific access, optional expiry, revocation and a visible warning that recipients can forward access. Test the unauthenticated recipient experience before calling it private.

6. **SEVERITY: HIGH — The core bedtime promise collapses when generation is slow, interrupted or fails.** The proposal sells a first page in under three minutes and pages appearing as they are drawn, but gives no timeout, recovery, partial-book policy or alternative for a child waiting in bed. The current reader already distinguishes building, paused and missing-art states; the redesign must not erase them. **Evidence:** [VISION.md](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/VISION.md:63), [BookView.jsx](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/src/pages/BookView.jsx:687). **Fix:** Design queued, slow, disconnected, partially complete and failed states. Save the request, explain whether it will continue, prevent duplicate submissions, offer retry and immediately offer a previously approved book. Measure actual first-readable-page times on Israeli mobile connections before advertising a target.

7. **SEVERITY: HIGH — Five free stories per month and a nightly creation prompt are in tension.** The prominent raised “new story” button encourages a paid or quota-consuming action every night, while the product promises a recurring ritual. “Gift” language obscures whether retries, regeneration, a failed run or a dedication page use quota or cost money. A separate $1.90 cap may also conflict with the apparent guarantee of five stories unless its behavior is defined. **Evidence:** [a-home.html](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/mockups/a-home.html:82), [VISION.md](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/VISION.md:80). **Fix:** Make “continue” and “read again” first-class nightly actions. Before creation, show remaining stories, reset date, any price and exactly when quota is charged; explicitly define failed runs and retries. Do not promise five if a separate cap can prevent five.

8. **SEVERITY: HIGH — Offline is an illustration, not a usable fallback.** An empty-state paper boat does nothing when Wi-Fi drops at bedtime. The vision does not say which books, images, narration or reading positions work without a connection. **Evidence:** [DESIGN-DIRECTION.md](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/DESIGN-DIRECTION.md) proposes an offline illustration; the current reader fetches book and page data in [BookView.jsx](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/src/pages/BookView.jsx:155). **Fix:** Specify and test a parent-initiated “available offline” download for selected approved books, including text, images and any licensed audio; show download status and storage use. If this cannot be built, state the limitation and offer an immediate online fallback rather than implying offline reading works.

9. **SEVERITY: HIGH — The family model is a single-child demo dressed as a household product.** A greets one child, preselects yesterday’s heroes and offers one remembered book. That fails when siblings alternate phones, read together, have different ages or do not want the same child featured. Grandpa is only a link recipient, though the vision also calls grandparents creators. **Evidence:** [a-home.html](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/mockups/a-home.html:75), [VISION.md](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/VISION.md:71). **Fix:** Let an adult choose who is reading tonight, select multiple children, set the audience age and switch saved progress per reader or shared session. Specify whether another adult can create, view or only receive an individual book; do not quietly expose the whole family library.

10. **SEVERITY: HIGH — The reader is the product, yet neither direction designs it.** The supplied visuals show a dashboard and only wizard step one. They do not establish whether a three-year-old can identify a book, turn a page without accidental taps, pause narration, or recover when the phone rotates. Textless navigation is asserted, not demonstrated. A horizontal shelf with hidden scrollbars is also an undiscoverable gesture for some children. **Evidence:** [a-home.html](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/mockups/a-home.html:27), [VISION.md](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/VISION.md:16). **Fix:** Prototype and test the actual reader with children at 3–4, 5–6 and 7–8 alongside parents: generous separated previous/next controls, obvious play/pause, replay, progress and a calm end. Use recognisable illustrated covers, not colour alone; offer a visible alternative to swiping.

11. **SEVERITY: HIGH — The first demo’s narration cannot honestly work as described for arbitrary Hebrew names.** A prerecorded narration cannot pronounce any newly typed name unless the name is omitted, spliced from a limited library, or generated by speech technology. Hebrew names can also be ambiguous without vowels or pronunciation guidance. Automatic audio may be unwelcome in a quiet bedroom. **Evidence:** [VISION.md](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/VISION.md:58). **Fix:** Label the first book as a sample, start audio only after a tap, and either narrate generic wording while displaying the name or explicitly budget and test dynamic speech with a pronunciation correction. Do not present a templated illustration as a likeness of the child.

12. **SEVERITY: MED — Direction A is the better choice, but its current magic is generic.** The moon, scattered dots, purple gradient, glowing gold CTA and anonymous smiling avatars could belong to almost any AI bedtime app. The book covers are coloured rectangles with moons and hills; they do not express a family, a story or Sipurai. **Evidence:** [a-home.html](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/mockups/a-home.html:13), [a-home.html](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/mockups/a-home.html:90), [A mobile screenshot](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/mockups/shots/a-home-375-fold.png). **Fix:** Choose **A** for its clearer continue-reading hierarchy and calmer parent shell. Give it a commissioned Hebrew storybook identity: a distinctive illustrated family shelf, recognisable recurring characters, a tactile page motif and a small set of original visual details drawn from the family’s own stories. Test it with actual generated covers before locking the system.

13. **SEVERITY: MED — Direction B is louder and less strategically focused, not automatically more child-friendly.** The dominant new-story tile, dotted background, tilted stickers, thick outlines and decorative sun compete for attention. The language shifts from “read tonight” to “create an adventure”; the first action is creation even when a book is waiting. A bold outline does not compensate for a missing reader design. **Evidence:** [b-home.html](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/mockups/b-home.html:50), [B mobile screenshot](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/mockups/shots/b-home-375-fold.png). **Fix:** Do not adopt B’s whole shell. Borrow its more legible, concrete character selection where testing supports it; put colour and delight into the illustrations and a restrained optional celebration, with motion and sound off by default at bedtime.

14. **SEVERITY: MED — The navigation is tidy on paper and repetitive on the phone.** A has a new-story action in the hero and a raised permanent new-story tab, while hero management occupies a permanent tab despite being an occasional task. “Parents” becomes a drawer for profiles, privacy, billing, orders, language, help and accessibility. The bell adds an undefined interruption. **Evidence:** [a-home.html](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/mockups/a-home.html:67), [a-home.html](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/mockups/a-home.html:123), [VISION.md](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/VISION.md:28). **Fix:** Test a smaller navigation centred on Tonight and Shelf, with creation contextual and family management within the adult area. Remove the bell until notifications have a demonstrated job. Give billing, privacy, help and orders clear labels inside Parents, with a direct help route available during errors.

15. **SEVERITY: MED — The studio is both overpromised and buried.** Moving the advanced editor inside an existing book blocks the power user who wants to start with deliberate scene planning or model choice. Conversely, promising “every capability we pay for” while hiding model names deprives knowledgeable users of meaningful control and cost information. A list of model options is not proof that each works end to end. **Evidence:** [VISION.md](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/VISION.md:17), [VISION.md](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/VISION.md:42), [ModelSelector.jsx](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/src/components/ai/ModelSelector.jsx:83). **Fix:** Offer a clearly labelled adult “start in studio” entry as well as opening an existing book. Inventory and test actual supported capabilities; show task-oriented defaults, with provider/model, availability and cost visible in an advanced disclosure where those choices affect the result.

16. **SEVERITY: HIGH — Existing persistence and routing do not automatically support the promised flows.** Wizard autosave is local, waits two seconds, starts only after a topic, idea or title, and silently skips storage errors. Reading position is also stored on the current device. Therefore “your choices are saved” and seamless continuation across devices or adults are not established. Renaming routes also requires redirects that preserve identifiers and query parameters, especially existing public book links. **Evidence:** [BookWizard.jsx](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/src/pages/BookWizard.jsx:181), [BookView.jsx](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/src/pages/BookView.jsx:166), [App.jsx](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/src/App.jsx:48), [VISION.md](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/VISION.md:54). **Fix:** Define device-only versus account-synced persistence, save hero-only drafts, expose save failures and test interruption, sign-in handoff, cross-device use and every legacy shared-link shape. Preserve working incremental generation, RTL navigation and read-aloud during migration.

17. **SEVERITY: MED — Accessibility is treated as button size and reduced motion.** The mockups use small, low-contrast secondary text; A places checkboxes inside a `radiogroup`, while its photo switch is a non-focusable `span`. A’s character likeness guarantee is stated without evidence. Large touch targets alone do not make a screen usable with a screen reader, enlarged text or motor impairments. **Evidence:** [a-wizard.html](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/mockups/a-wizard.html:60), [a-wizard.html](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/mockups/a-wizard.html:80), [a.css](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/mockups/a.css:47). **Fix:** Use a labelled checkbox group and a real switch button; verify focus order, Hebrew screen-reader announcements, contrast, 200% text scaling, safe areas and touch spacing on actual phones. Preserve English and Yiddish routes and functionality while fixing the audited translation gaps.

18. **SEVERITY: MED — “The illustrations will remember” promises a consistency the code and mockup do not prove.** A model sheet is an art-direction goal, not a guarantee that every image model will preserve identity, clothing, age or family resemblance across pages. For a personalised product, visible inconsistency damages trust faster than an ordinary generic picture. **Evidence:** [a-wizard.html](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/mockups/a-wizard.html:58), [DESIGN-DIRECTION.md](/root/work/sipurai-ux-49eb82ff/docs/ux-overhaul/DESIGN-DIRECTION.md). **Fix:** Change the promise to “we’ll try to keep their look consistent,” let parents approve and correct the character reference, and test multi-page consistency for different ages, skin tones, hair, disabilities and multiple heroes before advertising it as a capability.

**Review limit:** This is a source and static-mockup review, including visual inspection of the four 375px fold screenshots. I did not run the app, test real generated books, validate backend privacy enforcement, or observe children and parents using it.

**VERDICT: Choose direction A as a starting point; reject the proposal for implementation until the reader, child safety, family sharing, quota and failure states are designed and tested.**
